## Suggested store description

Grow a garden that's yours! Roll 84 tree parts, mix trunks, leaves, roots and fruit, and discover 420 part-and-mutation combinations. Grow your next tree while your orchard keeps producing, collect and sell fruit, and unlock all six garden plots with coins.

Your third roll guarantees Rare or better. Visit Tree Parts → Journal for a free daily roll, gentle daily goals, collection rewards and garden visits. Join Golden Rain, Windstorm and the cooperative Pest Raid to earn keepsakes. Ascend for permanent Golden Saplings and a second growth pad.

All core progression is available free. Optional purchases speed up progress or add convenience/cosmetics. Exact current odds are shown in Odds. This description is a draft and should be used only once all release checks are completed.
